Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

This policy explains how EP Capital Trading LLC ("we", "us") handles information in our iPhone apps and on this website. It applies to every app we publish on the Apple App Store unless an app has its own policy.

What stays on your device

We design our apps to keep personal content on your iPhone. This includes journal entries, habit and streak data, check-ins, and the apps and websites you choose to block. Where an app offers AI features, they run on your device using Apple's built-in models, and your content is not sent to us.

Screen Time data

Apps that use Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework only receive the selections you make, in a form Apple protects. We cannot see which apps you use or how long you use them, and we never collect, sell or share this information.

Information we may collect

  • Purchases: Apple processes all payments. We receive confirmation that a subscription is active, but never your name, card or billing details.
  • Diagnostics: If you agree to share them through your iPhone settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.
  • Support emails: If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and we use it only to reply.
  • Optional sync: If an app offers syncing or sharing (for example, with a partner), that data is stored in your iCloud account or on a secured server only to provide the feature, and you can delete it at any time.

What we don't do

We do not sell personal information, we do not use third-party advertising trackers, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

You can delete your data at any time by deleting the app or using the app's delete option. California residents and others may request access to or deletion of any information we hold by emailing us.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
