Support

Need help with one of our apps? Email us at info@epcapitaltrading.com and include the app name, your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose the app and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

How do I get a refund?

Apple processes all payments and refunds. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I reinstalled the app or got a new phone. How do I restore my subscription?

Open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

Why does the app ask for Screen Time access?

Our blocker uses Apple's Screen Time framework to pause or block the apps and websites you choose. Apple does not share which apps you use with us, and that information stays on your device.

Need urgent help with gambling?

In the U.S., call or text 1-800-GAMBLER, available 24/7. Our apps are self-help tools and are not a substitute for professional treatment.
